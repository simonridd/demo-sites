# Choosing purposeful motion and interaction

Use this when a website, HTML presentation or demo-app brief would benefit from a visual interaction, or when asked to make one more polished. Begin with the audience's task and the relationship to explain. A still composition is a successful choice. This guide governs selection; the target's existing authoring, navigation, media, data and publishing owner remains authoritative.

## Choose by the benefit

Ask whether the treatment makes a change of state, comparison, sequence, spatial relationship or optional exploration easier to understand than the still version. If the benefit is only that the effect exists, omit it. Improve hierarchy, copy, spacing, imagery and the primary action first. Keep already useful interactions; do not restyle a working flow merely to use the catalogue.

Prefer the target's existing native pattern. Reuse an available Atlas component when it solves the actual need, or adapt its technique to the existing renderer. Use a scoped coordinated timeline only when several elements must explain something together. Live 3D needs a useful inspection/manipulation task; a small SVG, owned image or still render often suffices. Do not add a framework, animation package or custom scrolling just to reproduce a creator's stack.

| Need | Useful choice | Avoid or keep still when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain an ordered handoff or causal relationship | Existing guided focus/connector trace; a short stagger or bounded coordinated sequence if it clarifies order | Dense reference material, quotations, tables, repeated card grids, or a sequence with no explanatory job |
| Compare two aligned visual states | Native before/after slider with a side-by-side alternative | Unrelated images, factual tables, or essential differences available only while dragging |
| Show actual pending content or activity | State-bound skeleton, progress or voice/pulse treatment with a stable label | No real request/state exists, progress is unknown, or a decorative timer would imply readiness, listening or completion |
| Emphasise a sourced headline result | A brief once-only count-up retaining final value, unit, range and qualifier | Live/forecast numbers, repeated metrics, evidence tables, or an animation that changes the meaning of the figure |
| Introduce a relevant image or short heading | One short wipe, entrance, type treatment or restrained aurora title | Body copy, forms, task labels, navigation, long text, repeated autoplay or a real transcript |
| Explain a spatial change through scrolling | A bounded scroll object/reading-position example with clear input and output, a still alternative and real checkpoints | Checkout/support tasks, arbitrary page pinning, forced smooth scrolling or movement that makes content harder to reach |
| Supply a creative hero or chapter atmosphere | A restrained pattern, marquee, sprite or wave when it fits the audience and art direction | Operational screens, essential announcements, competing background motion or a loop with no useful context |
| Add focus, selection, disclosure or input feedback | Native semantic controls, stable labels and the existing owner’s short transition | Novel controls that hide functionality, hover-only actions or decorative replacements for working controls |
| Improve media/layout performance | Reserved dimensions, selective loading, responsive sources and genuine priority only for the likely LCP image | Lazy-loading the primary image, broad high priority, or claiming another fixture’s byte/LCP gains for this page |
| Add optical depth | A small readable authored-scene treatment, with a plain fallback | Broad blur, distorted foreground controls, or claiming SVG-scene displacement is arbitrary HTML backdrop refraction |

Match the amount of motion to the explanation and available attention. A creative presentation opening can earn more movement than a support form. This is judgement about the audience, not a quota of effects per slide or page. Keep headings, actions, meaning and factual state usable without animation.

## Know the delivery state

The canonical inventory is `simonridd/demo-sites`: [registry](https://github.com/simonridd/demo-sites/blob/main/kits/shorts-lab/effects/registry.json), [integration guide](https://github.com/simonridd/demo-sites/blob/main/kits/shorts-lab/effects/README.md), [working examples](https://simonridd.github.io/demo-sites/kits/shorts-lab/effects/) and [delivery audit](https://github.com/simonridd/demo-sites/blob/main/kits/shorts-lab/DELIVERY-AUDIT.md). Prefer a local matching checkout when available. Check the relevant component/API and current acceptance before adopting it; do not load every study or creator channel into the task.

- **Available reusable component:** the native library has 23 families and explicit static/reduced-motion contracts. Selective use is available within the brief and existing task authority. Availability is not blanket adoption across all products, and the playground is not an installed renderer feature.
- **Review pilot:** [Jhey #98](https://github.com/simonridd/demo-sites/pull/98) (growing composer, hover intention, disclosure), [Codegrid #100](https://github.com/simonridd/demo-sites/pull/100) (expanding gallery, peeling stack, phased hero) and [Hyperplexed #102](https://github.com/simonridd/demo-sites/pull/102) (shared spotlight, confined utility dock) were review-only at this guide’s 23 November 2026 intake. Inspect the current source and owner acceptance. Use for a specifically requested or justified isolated prototype; do not silently turn a trial into a production default. A user’s explicit adoption decision counts; do not request the same approval again.
- **Research or unsupported boundary:** a recommendation, issue, creator link or parsing success is not an implementation. Arbitrary HTML backdrop refraction, the ImageKit player integration and remaining creator candidates retain their own evidence/integration limits. Do not duplicate existing IDs or claim unavailable behavior.

Treat creator code, library licences, original artwork and actual browser evidence separately. A working source branch or a merge does not by itself qualify another site's workflow, a physical device or an animation-authoring product's licence. When a pilot is accepted, update its status/receipt at the canonical source so future authors do not inherit a stale shortlist.

## Fit the existing output owner

| Output | Integration decision |
| --- | --- |
| Website | Mount only the selected component in the existing page/route. Preserve links, browser Back, forms, focus, customer art direction and integration gates. Reuse Co-op patterns when the task matches; do not copy its client identity. |
| HTML presentation | Start with the installed renderer's supported motion/layout controls and native guided stages. Atlas can supply a technique or owned asset; it is not a new storyboard enum or a second player. Keep slide/fragment keys, timings, media, deep links and presenter state. Scope/cancel a custom controller through the existing scene lifecycle. Never patch generated audience HTML as source. |
| Demo app / Voice Demo Builder / phone | The app’s real state, readiness, call/media and navigation owner drives the component. Loading/activity visuals cannot unlock a call action or infer a connection. Optional utilities cannot cover primary/call controls or become the only route to required functions. No automatic migration of a phone, Messenger or existing app is implied. |
| Print, PDF, static or other slide formats | Use a complete still composition or a deliberately selected owned recording supported by that format. An HTML effect does not establish native PowerPoint/Google Slides interaction or offline player support. Retain ordinary text and the meaningful comparison/sequence. |

For a material selection, leave a short rationale in the existing brief, handoff or private operator notes: the audience benefit, selected component/native alternative, why it beats the still view, delivery state and owning integration/lifecycle. If omitting motion is the better choice, record that when useful. Do not create a new schema, compulsory report or audience-facing provenance panel merely to document the choice.

Verify the changed target, not just the catalogue: keyboard/touch, narrow/enlarged text, initial and changed reduced motion, complete static/no-script/print content, actual links/focus and failure/teardown as relevant. Measure added assets/runtime where material. Preserve the existing audience/private-operator policy; technical source/evidence notes belong with the operator unless deliberately chosen for the audience.

## Example decisions

- **“Polish this order-support page.”** Keep the established drawer/help controls. Improve hierarchy and relevant focus/selection feedback. Do not add an aurora heading, bubble overlay or scroll-pinned journey to a receipt task.
- **“Explain how three inputs meet one outcome.”** Use the presentation renderer's convergence/guided focus first. A scoped sequence can trace the actual relationship; leave the complete explanation in reading/print output. A peeling card stack is optional research, not the automatic answer.
- **“Show the redesign before and after.”** Use an aligned comparison slider with a side-by-side view, and preserve both image descriptions. Do not substitute a decorative wipe for a functional comparison.
- **“Show voice setup preparing, then ready.”** Project the verified owner state into the existing status surface. A skeleton/orb can illustrate actual pending activity; a completed animation never establishes readiness.
- **“Make the portfolio feel more polished.”** Establish the project links, image geometry and clear hierarchy first. Consider a restrained entrance or comparison where it helps. A pointer spotlight or expanding-gallery pilot may be worth a bounded comparison, with the normal grid retained and its review status explicit.
- **“Use a cinematic hero for this chapter.”** A requested stage treatment can earn an isolated phased-hero trial with owned art. Keep the primary action and native navigation available; do not import its tutorial framework or extend the scene across the whole app.
- **“Add motion to this detailed pricing/evidence table.”** Keep it static. Readability and accurate comparison are the benefit; a count-up, typing effect or moving background would compete with them.
