Production at a glance

Genesys virtual agent

Give the virtual agent a clear job

The official Genesys AVA skills cover design, build, tests, evaluation and review.

Design the conversation

Define the agent’s role, what it may do, what it should say and when to hand over.

Test real choices

Run scenarios that check the conversation, tool calls and agreed outcomes.

Benefit

Find weak replies, missed steps and bad handoffs before relying on the agent in a session.

Environment Builder

Route the contact

Use Architect flows, queues and Messenger to get voice or digital contacts to the right place.

Give people the right information

Use Data Actions for lookups, Knowledge for answers, and Agent Copilot for checklists and summaries.

Benefit

Review the planned changes, then read back what was applied so the setup can be checked and reused.

Recording and playback

Turn a working journey into a guided replay

Recording and playback skills turn captured steps into an experience others can follow.

Capture the important moment

Record the customer and agent views, then add cues where a decision or handoff happens.

Guide the viewer

Use click targets, chapters and synced audio where it helps. Assemble selected journeys for a session.

Benefit

A presenter can revisit the same moment, and colleagues can explore it in their own time.

Presentations and sites

Presentation authoring

Demo sites

Benefit

A worked example

A footwear exchange, from call to follow-up

A customer needs the same trainers in a different size. Here is how the skills work together.

  1. Prepare the call

    Studio gives the customer a clear reason to call and the presenter a consistent starting point.

  2. Set up agent support

    Genesys routes the call. Copilot can surface the exchange checklist and relevant guidance.

  3. Show the result

    Show the agreed next step and check the after-call summary. Capture the key moments for replay.
