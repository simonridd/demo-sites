A story with a clear next step.

The thin bar shows document scroll position. It does not measure comprehension, progress in a task, loading or completion.

Start with a person

This original fictional article is a layout fixture. Give the reader a clear situation and a reason to continue. Keep meaningful text available in the document, with or without JavaScript.

Keep the useful detail

Describe what the person needs to do and what information helps. A quiet position indicator can orient a reader through a longer page; it should never obscure navigation.

Leave room for judgement

When a choice matters, make the options clear. A scrolling animation cannot establish that an action was taken, a record saved or an outcome verified.

Finish the scene

Carry the person’s need into a concrete next step. This is the end of the fixture, not proof that any real workflow is complete.
