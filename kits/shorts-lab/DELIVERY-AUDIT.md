### Jhey scan and issue 96 reconciliation

The subsequent issue 96 refines the first trial to content sizing, hover intention/safety and native disclosures. These supersede the preliminary navigation-first ordering below; the remaining ranked drawer, progressive-blur, table-highlighting and section-crossfade candidates remain research. Inspected the live index and these linked prototype pages on 23 November. This is source discovery and triage, not a visual/browser qualification or a source-code/licence review; no creator assets/code are copied.

- [Scroll-target anchored navigation](https://www.jhey.dev/prototypes/2025/scroll-target-anchored-nav/index.html) and [TOC scroll markers](https://www.jhey.dev/prototypes/2025/toc-scroll-markers/index.html): **research next** as one active-section/navigation pattern for long documentation, multi-section demos or presentation handouts. Preserve real anchors, keyboard navigation and scroll-margin; active state must follow actual sections. Existing header/progress studies are the starting point.
- [Field sizing](https://www.jhey.dev/prototypes/2026/field-sizing/index.html): **research next** for growing message/composer textareas, with a maximum height, native scroll fallback and stable surrounding controls. Extend the existing input study if accepted.
- [Anchor hover cards](https://www.jhey.dev/prototypes/2026/anchor-hover-cards/index.html): **conditional research** for optional previews only. Need real focus/touch disclosure and dismissal before reuse; never hide essential actions behind hover.
- [Adaptive radius](https://www.jhey.dev/prototypes/2026/adaptive-radius/index.html): **overlap**, evaluate within existing corner/gutter guidance; no separate effect needed.
- `lazy-gallery`, number-roll/dial and decorative glass/sprite examples in the index: **overlap or stage research**, not new product defaults. Existing 47/20/75/60/23 already cover the underlying needs.

This records the previously unlogged ideas. It does not claim all creator videos or 867 index prototypes were reviewed. Issue 96 now retains the source-specific shortlist and its review gate. Further source-specific requests should enter this ledger with a benefit, existing-ID mapping and acceptance criteria before implementation.
